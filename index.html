Drinking water quality dashboard

Weekly drinking-water surveillance results from the KMAM Water Quality Surveillance System (WQS), checked against the Ministry of Health National Drinking Water Quality Standard.

Load your data

Add the Excel files you exported from WQS. You can add several files at once, for example one per plant, and they are combined. Everything stays on this computer; nothing is uploaded to the internet.

Files that work

  • WQS Excel exports, with sheets for Group 1 (bacteriology and physical), Group 2 (general chemistry), Group 3 (heavy metals and THM) and Group 4. Each sheet needs the columns Stn.No., Type and Smp. Date.
  • The combined workbook (KMAM_Tuaran_Pensampelan_Air_2025-2026.xlsx) with its Loji and Tahun columns.
  • Plants are named from the Loji column, or from the course code in Stn.No. (for example 1223C001 is Topokon 1). Unknown course codes are shown by code.
  • PDF printouts cannot be read here. Export them as Excel from WQS, or send the PDFs to Claude to convert.

Where the samples are taken

Water travels from the river, through the treatment plant (LRA), to people's taps. Samples are taken at four points along the way. Tap a point to filter the dashboard to it.

Treatment plant (LRA) Filtering and chlorination
Raw waterTreated water: "Treated water (all)" covers these three
Why fluoride is 0%. Sabah stopped adding fluoride to public water supplies in 1989 over concerns about supervising the dosing, so the Tuaran plants supply non-fluoridated water. The 0 readings reflect this state policy, not a fault at the plants. Most of Peninsular Malaysia fluoridates within the Ministry of Health range of 0.4–0.6 mg/l, although Pahang stopped in 2018. Without fluoridated water, fluoride toothpaste and fluoride care at dental clinics matter more for preventing tooth decay. Ordinary carbon water filters leave fluoride in the water; reverse-osmosis filters remove it.

Notes

  • Problem codes C and Z mark a test that was not done because of an instrument problem (C at KKM, Z at the Chemistry Department, JKM). Colour and the HoCl+NH3 chlorine test carry code C in almost every sample, so they were not measured.
  • Treated water means every station type other than raw intake: plant outlet (TPO), service reservoir (SRO) and distribution (A).
  • Limits for treated water follow Table 2 of the national standard; for raw intake, the raw-water acceptable values in Table 1.
  • Temperature recorded as 0 °C (18 samples on 10 June 2025) is treated as missing.
  • Topokon 1 raw water on 13 January 2026 has aluminium recorded as 17 mg/l, far above every other reading. It is kept as entered.
  • 2026 values come from printed pages of the surveillance system (WQS). Telibong 1 bacteriology after 3 June 2026 was not available.
  • Plant locations are approximate, from public map listings. Telibong 1 and 2 share one site, as do Topokon 1 and 2; their markers are offset slightly so both can be tapped.
